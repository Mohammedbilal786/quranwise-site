What this policy covers

This policy covers this website, quranwise.app. The Quranwise app has not been released yet. Before it is, we will update this page to explain what the app collects and why.

What we collect

  • Your email address, if you choose to type it into the “Notify me” box.
  • Anything you send us by email, if you write to us.

That is all. This website does not use advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not set cookies.

Why we collect it

We use your email address for one purpose: to tell you when Quranwise is ready. We rely on your consent, which you give by signing up and can withdraw at any time. If you email us, we use what you send to reply to you.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who helps us handle it

We use a small number of service providers to run this website:

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) receives the sign-up form and passes your email address on to our mailbox.
  • Our email provider stores the messages in our mailbox.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. Like most web hosts, it records technical details such as visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes.

Some of these providers are based outside the United Kingdom, so your information may be processed in other countries. We share your information with no one else, unless the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address until you ask us to remove it, or until we no longer need it to tell you about the launch.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • tell you what information we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct it if it is wrong;
  • delete it, or take you off the list.

To do any of these, email salaam@quranwise.app. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

The sign-up on this website is meant for adults. If you believe a child has given us their email address, tell us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update this page and the date at the top.
